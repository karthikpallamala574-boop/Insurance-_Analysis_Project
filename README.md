**Insurance Analysis Project >**
**
Overview**

Business teams generate a large amount of data every week, but turning that data into meaningful insights is often a challenge. This project focuses on analyzing branch-level business performance and presenting key metrics in a single interactive dashboard.

The dashboard helps management monitor targets, achievements, invoicing performance, sales opportunities, and employee activities. Instead of reviewing multiple reports, decision-makers can quickly assess branch performance, identify revenue opportunities, and track progress toward business goals.

---

**The Business Challenge**

For organizations operating across multiple branches, performance data is often scattered across different reports. This makes it difficult to answer important questions such as:

- Are branches meeting their business targets?
- How much revenue is being generated through new business, renewals, and cross-selling activities?
- Which opportunities are most likely to generate revenue?
- How active are account executives in client engagement?
- Where are the biggest gaps between targets and actual performance?

The objective of this project was to create a centralized reporting solution that provides clear visibility into branch performance and sales activities.

---
**
Dashboard Objectives**

The dashboard was designed to help stakeholders:

- Track branch performance on a weekly basis
- Compare targets against actual achievements
- Monitor invoice generation and revenue realization
- Analyze sales opportunities across different stages
- Evaluate account executive performance
- Identify high-value business opportunities
- Support data-driven decision making

---

**Key Metrics Tracked**

Business Performance

- Cross Sell Target vs Achievement
- New Business Target vs Achievement
- Renewal Target vs Achievement
- Invoice Achievement Percentage
- Revenue Achievement Percentage

Opportunity Analysis

- Total Opportunities
- Open Opportunities
- Opportunity Revenue
- Opportunity Funnel Analysis
- Product-wise Opportunity Distribution

Employee Performance

- Meetings Conducted by Account Executive
- Invoice Contribution by Account Executive
- Yearly Meeting Count

---

**Dashboard Highlights**

Target vs Achievement Analysis

The dashboard compares business targets against actual achievements across:

- Cross Sell Business
- New Business
- Renewal Business

This helps management quickly identify areas that are exceeding expectations and areas that require attention.

Opportunity Pipeline Monitoring

A dedicated opportunity section provides visibility into:

- Open opportunities
- Opportunity revenue
- Opportunity stages
- Top revenue-generating opportunities

This allows teams to focus on deals with the highest business impact.

Account Executive Performance

Employee-level performance metrics help track:

- Meeting activities
- Invoice generation
- Revenue contributions

These insights can be used to recognize top performers and identify coaching opportunities.

Revenue Funnel Analysis

The stage funnel visualizes how opportunities move through different stages of the sales process, helping stakeholders understand potential revenue leakage and conversion effectiveness.

---
**
Key Insights Generated**

Through this analysis, stakeholders can:

- Monitor branch performance in real time.
- Identify branches exceeding or missing targets.
- Track business growth through cross-sell, renewal, and new business activities.
- Prioritize high-value opportunities.
- Evaluate sales team engagement and productivity.
- Improve forecasting and revenue planning.
